## What this event was

The annual **AWS Summit** in Mexico City. A full day at Centro Citibanamex with a partner expo, keynote, 120+ technical sessions, workshops, and demos. The largest AWS event in Mexico.

Classic summit format: morning sessions, expo floor all day, midday keynote, networking at the close.

## Topics covered

- Authorization and identity for AI agents with access to production
- Event-driven architecture for agent workflows
- Amazon Bedrock, agent memory, tools, and guardrails
- Amazon S3 Vectors
- Observability and cloud security
- How companies are applying AI to real products and workflows

## Takeaways

- I liked seeing AWS Community Builders share practical knowledge with other developers. The community sessions made a large conference feel more open and useful.
- Seeing how different companies apply AI made the ideas more concrete. Each company had different data, limits, and business needs, so there was no single approach that worked for everyone.
