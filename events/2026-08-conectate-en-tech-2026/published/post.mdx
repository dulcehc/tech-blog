## What this event was

Conéctate en Tech calls itself the largest gathering for women in technology in Mexico. The 2026 edition was an afternoon on the 40th floor of Sofitel Reforma with fireside chats, masterclasses, an unfiltered panel, and networking with drinks.

It was not a deep technical conference. It was a space for career growth, real stories, and connection between leaders. The names linked here are some of the speakers; the lineup was larger than this list.

## Topics covered

- **AI without fear**: using AI as a career copilot (Anna Lagos)
- **Five years connecting the future of tech**: fireside with Ana Paula Padilla, Carolina Arreola, and Lorenza Lanz
- **UNFILTERED panel**: honest conversations on growth with Carolina Avella, Lorena Ostos, Giovanna Abramo, and Astrid Espinosa
- **Leadership with intention**: masterclass with María Teresa Arnal

## Takeaways

- Hearing women leaders speak honestly about leading in tech was valuable. They shared the challenges they faced, what helped them move forward, and the advice they would give others.
- Anna Lagos showed practical ways to use AI as a career coach. Her prompts focused on getting direct feedback, making career decisions, and preparing for difficult conversations.
- The UNFILTERED panel focused on what happens when things do not go as planned. The speakers shared how they faced setbacks and kept building their careers.
- María Teresa Arnal gave a strong masterclass on building a career that feels right for you instead of trying to create an image for other people.
- I love spaces where people can speak openly about any topic, share their experiences and challenges, and know there is a community ready to support one another.
