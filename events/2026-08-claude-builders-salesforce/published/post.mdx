## What this event was

A meetup hosted by **Salesforce Mexico**, in lab format. You connected Claude to a Salesforce dev org and built a working flow in about 75 minutes.

It coincided with the **Claudeforce** announcement, the Salesforce and Anthropic partnership integrating Claude into the CRM ecosystem.

## Topics covered

- AI agents connected to real business data
- Claude + Salesforce integration (dev org provided)
- Hands-on lab with three tracks by level

## Takeaways

- Even though I do not use Salesforce, I was curious to see how Claude works in its ecosystem. Hearing practical use cases from the panelists and then applying those ideas in the lab made the experience meaningful.
