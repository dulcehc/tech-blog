## What this event was

The first CDMX edition of **Claude for Healthcare**, organized by the Claude community and hosted at **Novartis Mexico**. An evening for people in health, pharma, hospitals, healthtech, and clinical data, with real cases and no vendor pitch.

Part of the [claudecommunity.mx](https://claudecommunity.mx) ecosystem, which groups vertical meetups across Mexico.

## Topics covered

- Using AI to support diagnosis and clinical decisions
- Giving patients clearer access to health information
- Detecting and reviewing possible medicine side effects
- Claude for pharmacovigilance (TMC)
- Building agent systems for pharmaceutical work
- Trust, safety, and compliance in healthcare AI

## Takeaways

- I saw more practical ways AI is being used in healthcare, including tools that can support diagnosis and help people make better clinical decisions.
- AI can help teams detect and review possible side effects from medicines by turning unstructured information into clear, verifiable summaries.
- It was useful to see how Novartis builds agent systems within the strict guidelines of the pharmaceutical industry. Safety, trust, compliance, and human review have to be part of the system from the start.
