## What this event was

WomenCon 2026 was a cybersecurity and innovation conference in Mexico City, organized by [WomenCon](https://www.linkedin.com/company/womencon) and hosted at [IQsec](https://www.linkedin.com/company/iqsec-ciberseguridad) (Av. Patriotismo 399).

## Topics covered

- Barriers women report in tech: unclear career paths, lack of mentors, explicit bias
- Ticket classification and the human factor in breaches
- LOLBINs: attackers reusing native OS tools (PowerShell, certutil, WMIC)
- Programmer vs pentester vs blue team: three distinct security roles
- Attacker vs citizen asymmetry: attackers automate, scale, and use AI
- Post-quantum cybersecurity and resilient cloud architecture
- AI agent stacks in SOC work: the "Director of Agents" model
- Resilience 360°: governance, IT-OT, and strategy

## Takeaways

- I left curious about practical uses of AI in cybersecurity. The "Director of Agents" model showed one analyst coordinating agents for triage, containment, and correlation. Attackers also use AI and automation at scale, while defenders have to catch every attempt.
- Alicia Trejo shared [Joy Buolamwini's research](https://www.media.mit.edu/articles/artificial-intelligence-has-a-problem-with-gender-and-racial-bias-here-s-how-to-solve-it/) on how facial-analysis systems can misidentify women, especially darker-skinned women. It was a clear reminder that AI needs inclusive data and testing.
- Hearing attendees and speakers talk openly about racism and gender bias was important. They shared how their knowledge, experience, and leadership helped them build their careers despite those barriers.
- One slide on unclear career paths and the lack of mentors added context to the career advice shared during the sessions.
- The closing panel was the highlight. The speakers shared how they communicate risk and handle difficult situations as leaders.
